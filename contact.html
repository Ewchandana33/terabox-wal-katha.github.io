Contact the site operator

Use the repository's GitHub issue system to report:

  • a broken or unavailable link;
  • a misleading description;
  • a security or accessibility issue;
  • a copyright or privacy concern;
  • potentially illegal or unsafe material.
Open a GitHub issue

Information to include

  • the exact page URL;
  • the relevant bundle or link number;
  • a brief and accurate description of the problem;
  • the requested action;
  • supporting public information where appropriate.

Protect sensitive information

GitHub issues are generally public. Do not include identity documents, private addresses, passwords, payment details, personal telephone numbers or other sensitive information.

If private information is required, request a private communication method without publishing the sensitive information itself.

External platform reports

This website cannot remove a file directly from TeraBox or another third-party provider. Contact the destination platform through its official reporting process for removal from that platform.

Response expectations

Reports should contain enough information to identify and review the affected link. Incomplete, abusive, automated or clearly false reports may not receive a response.
