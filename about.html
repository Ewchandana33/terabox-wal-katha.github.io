What this site is

terabox video link is an independent directory. It collects links to third-party TeraBox share pages in one mobile-friendly place and pairs them with practical guides for opening, saving and troubleshooting shared files.

The directory is small on purpose. It is not a streaming service, a download site or a file host, and it does not try to rank or review the content behind each link. Its job is to point you to a TeraBox page and to give you the information you need to use that page safely.

What this site is not

  • It is not TeraBox, and it is not owned, approved or sponsored by TeraBox.
  • It does not store, upload or serve the files behind a link.
  • It does not ask for your password, payment details or personal documents.
  • It does not require an account, a subscription or an extra app.

How links are handled

  • HTTPS only. The page refuses to turn a bundle button into a working link unless the address is a valid HTTPS URL. Anything else stays marked as unavailable.
  • Opened in a new tab. External links open in a new tab with security attributes so the destination cannot control this page.
  • Review when added and when reported. A link is looked at before it is published and again whenever a report arrives. We do not claim that every link is checked continuously, which is why the guides explain what to do when one fails.
  • Honest status. A bundle that cannot be opened is shown as unavailable instead of being left to fail.
  • Quick removal. A link can be disabled while a complaint is examined. See the copyright and removal policy.

Content standards

This directory must not be used to find or share illegal, infringing, non-consensual, exploitative or underage material. Links to such material are removed when they are found or reported, and users are encouraged to report them to the platform and to the authorities. Only link to material that the person who shares it is entitled to share.

How the guides are written

The guides describe general behaviour of cloud-storage share links and the TeraBox app. TeraBox changes its limits, wording and offers from time to time, so where an exact number or screen name could be out of date we say so and point you to the app's own settings. If a guide is wrong or outdated, please tell us.

Start with the setup guide, then keep the troubleshooting guide and the safety guide handy.

Independence and advertising

The site is run by an independent operator and is free to use. It is supported by advertising: a small number of clearly labelled advertisements appear on the home page, away from the bundle buttons. The privacy and cookie policies explain what advertising partners may process, and no advertiser can pay to change what a guide says.

Contact and corrections

To report a dead link, a mistake in a guide, a security problem or a rights concern, use the contact page. The site operator is reachable through the repository's public issue tracker, so please do not post private information there.
