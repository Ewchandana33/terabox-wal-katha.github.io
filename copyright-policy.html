1. What this website does and does not do

terabox video link is a directory of links to pages that other people created on TeraBox. This website does not store, upload or stream the files behind those links.

Because the files stay on TeraBox, removing a link from this directory does not delete the file from TeraBox. It only stops this website from pointing to it.

2. Respect for rights

Only link to material that the person sharing it is entitled to share. Links to infringing, non-consensual, exploitative or underage material are not allowed and are removed when they are found or reported.

3. How to report a link

Send a report through the Contact page and include:

  • the bundle number or the exact page URL;
  • the full address of the TeraBox page you are reporting;
  • a description of the work or right concerned;
  • why you believe the link is infringing or unlawful;
  • whether you own the right or act for the owner;
  • the action you request.
Public reports: GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Do not post identity documents, private addresses, telephone numbers or other sensitive details. Ask for a private channel if they are needed.

4. How reports are handled

  1. The report is checked for enough detail to identify the link.
  2. The link may be disabled while the report is examined.
  3. The link is removed or corrected if the concern is confirmed.
  4. Further information may be requested where needed.

No fixed response time is guaranteed. Reports that involve an immediate safety risk are handled first.

5. Report to TeraBox as well

To have a file removed from TeraBox itself, you must use TeraBox's own reporting process. This website has no access to TeraBox accounts or servers and cannot delete a file stored there.

6. Disputing a removal

If you believe a link was disabled by mistake, contact us with the bundle number and a short explanation, including why you are entitled to share the material. The request will be reviewed, and a link may stay removed where the rights position remains unclear.

7. False or abusive reports

Please report in good faith. Clearly false, automated or abusive reports may be ignored, and making a knowingly false claim can have legal consequences.

8. Repeat problems

Sources that repeatedly lead to infringing or unlawful material may be removed from the directory permanently.
