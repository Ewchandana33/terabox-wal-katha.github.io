How rights holders and affected persons can request review or removal of a link.

Respect for rights

TeraBox Movie Zone does not knowingly intend to link to material that infringes copyright, privacy, publicity or other legal rights.

The website does not directly host third-party files. Nevertheless, links may be reviewed and removed when a sufficiently detailed complaint is received.

Information required

A removal request should include:

  1. Identification of the protected work, person or legal right involved.
  2. The exact URL on this website where the disputed link appears.
  3. The exact external destination URL, if available.
  4. A clear explanation of why removal is requested.
  5. Your authority to act for the affected person or rights holder.
  6. A reliable method for the operator to contact you privately.
  7. A statement that the supplied information is accurate and submitted in good faith.

Privacy warning

Do not post identity documents, signatures, private addresses, telephone numbers or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

Use the initial public report only to identify the affected page. If additional private information is necessary, ask the site operator for an appropriate private submission method.

Review process

The operator may temporarily disable a disputed link while reviewing a complaint. Additional information may be requested where ownership, authorization or the affected material cannot be identified.

Clearly illegal, non-consensual, exploitative or underage material should be reported immediately to the relevant platform and appropriate authorities in addition to notifying this site.

Destination removal

Removing a link from this website does not remove the underlying file from TeraBox or another provider.

For removal from an external platform, contact that platform through its official reporting procedure.

False or abusive reports

Do not knowingly submit false ownership claims, impersonate another person or misuse the reporting process to suppress lawful material.

Submit a request

Begin a removal request through the Contact page and include the exact URL requiring review.
